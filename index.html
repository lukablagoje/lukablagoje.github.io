Tradeoffs & mistakes

Tradeoffs I deliberately make

In regulated systems, I trade raw speed for reproducibility and lineage. When latency matters, I separate fast operational paths from governed reporting paths and document which decisions can use which.

Mistakes I avoid
  • Shipping “smart” models without monitoring and rollback
  • Letting tribal knowledge replace documentation
  • Over-engineering governance that blocks delivery
